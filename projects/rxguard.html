RxGuard

A fully synthetic PBM claims analytics dashboard that turns raw claim activity into governed cost, utilization, pharmacy operations, drug, adherence, and data-quality views.

The problem

Claims reporting needs one trusted definition for every number.

PBM reporting combines financial responsibility, utilization, claim disposition, drug attributes, and member-level measures. Small denominator or status-rule differences can produce conflicting client answers. RxGuard centralizes those rules in a documented analytical layer and exposes the same approved measures across every view and export.

Synthetic-data boundary

Every client, member, claim, cost, NDC-like code, and drug record is fictional. The project contains no PHI and does not represent a production PBM platform.

System architecture

Quality gates sit between source files and every published KPI.

05 / DELIVERDashboard + ExcelSix Streamlit pages and client reporting packs.

Engineering decisions

The design favors explicit rules over hidden dashboard logic.

Reconciliation is a first-class check

Claim-level validation confirms gross cost equals member plus plan payment within a documented cent-level tolerance, alongside duplicate, referential-integrity, date, amount, and status checks.

Measures are documented once

A versioned KPI dictionary defines paid-claim denominators, generic dispensing rate, rejection rate, cost per paid claim, and all dashboard filters.

Adherence claims are deliberately narrow

The PDC-style calculation is labeled as a portfolio demonstration. It omits measure-specific exclusions and enrollment rules and is not presented as a certified HEDIS measure.

Evidence & outputs

The repository connects analysis, operations, and delivery.

  • Executive, client, drug, claims-operations, adherence, and data-quality dashboard pages.
  • High-cost drug, therapeutic-class, brand-versus-generic, and unusual-increase analysis.
  • Formatted Excel client packs generated from the same approved metric layer.
  • Regression tests covering pipeline refreshes, KPIs, exports, and Streamlit page smoke tests.

Limits & next steps

A credible demonstration keeps its boundary visible.

  • Synthetic patterns do not establish real formulary, utilization, or adherence behavior.
  • The public demo has no real client access control or protected data.
  • Drug opportunity flags are illustrative and require clinical and formulary review before use.
  • A production release would add enrollment eligibility, stronger audit trails, role-based authorization, and independent measure validation.

Manufacturing Quality Control Tower

An end-to-end quality analytics system that transforms the real UCI SECOM semiconductor dataset into historian-style process data, governed yield KPIs, statistical investigation queues, and auditable reporting assets.

The problem

Manufacturing decisions require traceable signals, not disconnected charts.

The source contains timestamped pass/fail outcomes and hundreds of anonymized process measurements with substantial missingness. The project preserves each published observation, models the measurements as historian-style tags, and connects every quality KPI to validation and reconciliation evidence.

Evidence boundary

The dataset's signals are anonymized. Findings identify statistical associations with failed units; they do not establish physical root causes or causal process effects.

Quality investigation

Multiple methods rank signals for engineering review.

Yield and completeness baselines

The governed output reconciles 1,463 passes and 104 failures across 1,567 production records, while explicitly tracking 41,951 missing readings and a 4.54% missing-signal rate.

Statistical association analysis

Welch tests, Benjamini-Hochberg correction, and Cohen's d compare passing and failed units. Weighted logistic regression uses a chronological 80/20 holdout, while robust anomaly scores and three-sigma alerts create complementary review queues.

Source discrepancy preserved

The UCI page describes 591 features, while the downloaded matrix contains 590 measurement columns. The pipeline records the discrepancy as a warning instead of rewriting the source.

Governance & delivery

The repository includes operational evidence beyond the model.

  • PostgreSQL schema and verification queries for 924,530 historian-style readings.
  • Source-controlled Power BI project with governed KPI measures.
  • Excel quality-review workbook with monthly yield, signal review, alerts, exceptions, and reconciliation.
  • Lean Six Sigma DMAIC and root-cause investigation reports.
  • ERPNext import and REST payload assets with explicit non-production boundaries.
  • Automated tests and GitHub Actions validation against PostgreSQL 16.

Limits & next steps

A portfolio control tower is not a production process-control system.

  • Anonymized tags prevent physical interpretation without process-engineering metadata.
  • Three-sigma alerts are statistical screening rules, not approved engineering limits.
  • The model supports prioritization and does not prove that changing a signal will reduce defects.
  • A production release would add equipment context, approved control limits, streaming ingestion, drift monitoring, and validated ERP transactions.

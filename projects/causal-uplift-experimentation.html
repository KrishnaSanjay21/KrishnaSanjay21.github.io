Causal Uplift & Experimentation

A reproducible pipeline that measures whether a randomized treatment works, estimates who changes behavior because of treatment, and turns those estimates into a ranked targeting policy.

The problem

Conversion propensity is not the same as persuasion.

A high-propensity customer may buy with or without a promotion. Targeting that customer wastes margin. Uplift modeling instead asks which customers convert because they received treatment, preserving the randomized experiment as the source of causal evidence.

Decision principle

Model accuracy alone cannot validate an uplift policy. Evaluation uses observed outcomes from randomized treatment and control groups.

Methodology

Average effect establishes evidence; heterogeneous effect shapes action.

  1. Validate assignment

    Load a reproducible synthetic RCT or corrected Criteo data and confirm balanced randomized treatment assignment.

  2. Estimate the ATE

    Compare treatment and control conversion rates and calculate absolute lift, relative lift, a p-value, and a normal-approximation 95% interval.

  3. Fit response models

    Train separate regularized logistic regressions for treated and untreated observations.

  4. Calculate conditional uplift

    Subtract predicted control response from predicted treated response for each customer to produce a transparent CATE score.

  5. Evaluate the policy

    Rank customers into deciles and compare observed treatment-control differences through uplift curves and incremental conversions.

Engineering choices

A simple estimator keeps every decision inspectable.

The two-model design is intentionally transparent: both response functions are ordinary regularized logistic models and the uplift score has a direct definition. Reproducible synthetic data includes known heterogeneous effects, while the real-data adapter maps the corrected public Criteo schema into the same pipeline.

Automated checks

Tests verify randomization balance, a statistically detectable positive effect in the fixture, finite scores, stronger observed uplift in the top segment than the bottom, and a ten-point uplift curve.

Deliverables

Every stage produces a decision-ready artifact.

  • experiment_summary.json records conversion rates, lift, uncertainty, and significance.
  • uplift_segments.csv summarizes audience deciles and observed treatment response.
  • uplift_curve.csv supports policy and incremental-conversion visualization.
  • report.html combines experiment evidence with the targeting recommendation.

Limits & next steps

The local result is a method demonstration, not a campaign claim.

  • The default experiment is synthetic and must not be presented as a production promotion outcome.
  • Two-model estimates can be unstable where treatment and control response models extrapolate differently.
  • Production evaluation should reserve an untouched randomized test set and estimate policy value with uncertainty.
  • Causal forests, doubly robust learners, propensity diagnostics, and uplift calibration are important comparisons.
Next milestone

Add cross-fitted doubly robust estimation and budget-aware policy value so targeting is optimized against both incremental conversions and promotion cost.
